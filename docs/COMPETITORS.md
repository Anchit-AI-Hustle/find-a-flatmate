# Competitor Analysis

_Checked 12 November 2026 · Desk research from public pages only. Nothing here comes from signing in, installing
an app or talking to users._

**How to read this.** Every fact is what a public page showed on the date checked, with its URL. Vendor claims are
marked as claims. Play Store figures are from the India storefront (`hl=en_IN&gl=IN`); `L` is lakh, `Cr` is crore.
Review quotes are verbatim (typos kept) from each app's public Play Store reviews, newest first, with the review
date; reviewer and staff names are left out. "Could not verify" means the page did not load or did not say.
Opinions are labelled **Opinion**.

## 1. Landscape

Indian flat-seekers have four kinds of option, and none does the whole job. **Large portals** (NoBroker,
Housing.com, MagicBricks) have the supply and lakhs of ratings, but discovery is list-and-filter over property
cards and contact sits behind paid plans; recent 1-star reviews are about stale listings, brokers posing as owners
and sales calls. **Managed co-living operators** (Stanza Living, Zolo) sell a bed, not a flatmate; their recent
1-star reviews are about deposits and charges. **Flatmate.in** is the established flatmate-specific app (5L+
installs, Noida-based operator); its praise is almost all for human relationship managers and its complaints are
about paying before you can chat, inactive users and refunds. A **new wave of small compatibility-first products**
(Bunkd, 360 Flatmates, NestMate) makes nearly the same promise as ours: lifestyle questions, a match score, chat
only after mutual interest, no brokerage. Two of them market swipe cards. All are tiny on the evidence available:
Bunkd reports 14 room-seekers in Gurgaon and 25 in Noida, and the Bunkd and 360 FlatMates Android apps each show
"50+" downloads. Facebook groups and Reddit could not be loaded, so the informal channel is described only through
secondary sources.

## 3. Notes per competitor

All pages checked 12 Nov 2026. Play links take the form `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=<id>`.

### Flatmate.in — <https://flatmate.in> · Play id `in.flatmate`
- "Find Flat and Flatmates, Rooms on Rent, Shared Apartments". Think Straight IT LLP, Noida. Also sells rental
  agreements, tenant verification and "List Your PG".
- Gurgaon page (<https://www.flatmate.in/flatmates-in-gurgaon>): list of cards with first name, locality, rent,
  "Looking for Male/Female Roommate" and distance; tabs All / Rooms / Roommates / PG; gender filter; visible
  without signing in. Homepage claims "compatibility match percentages", a "secure chat feature", hidden phone
  numbers, teams for people searching the same locality, and an "RM Plan" (relationship managers search for you).
- Claims "Trusted & loved by million users" and "With 1000+ listings". Body copy is awkward ("possess the
  authority to search into your favored location").
- Play: 3.9 from 3,648 ratings, 5L+ downloads, updated 5 Nov 2026, contains ads. App Store India: 3.1 from 215
  ratings; one in-app purchase, "FlatMate.in Premium (One Month)", ₹499
  (<https://apps.apple.com/in/app/flatmate-find-roommate-rooms/id1360260090>).
- Reviews (40 newest, 17 Aug – 10 Nov 2026). 5-star reviews mostly thank a named relationship manager: "i was
  assigned a dedicated relationship manager" (24 Oct). 1-star: "useless app with inactive users" (10 Nov); "I
  wasn't allowed to have a conversation with anyone as soon as I started searching" (9 Nov); "very old data is
  shown" (6 Oct); "no way to see new listings or know what you've already viewed" (2 Oct); "they lie about the
  refund part" (26 Aug). A 4-star: "the app asks for premium to chat with people right from start" (30 Oct).

### 360 Flatmates — <https://flatmates.360ghar.com> · Play id `com.the360ghar.flatmates360`
- "Find Compatible Flatmates & Verified Rooms Across India". "We're live in Gurugram and Bangalore right now."
- "6-dimension compatibility scoring — sleep, cleanliness, food, guests, work, and lifestyle", plus "society vibe
  tags". Play listing adds "swipe-based matching", "map-based discovery" and listing by landlords and property
  managers.
- "Book visits in 2 taps — no WhatsApp ping-pong." "Chat that starts with context — every chat already carries
  the listing, the match score, and visit details."
- "every room is reviewed before it goes live. Real rent, real availability."; "phone OTP, profile checks, and
  in-app reporting". "Searching and matching is 100% free. Optional paid plans exist for priority listings."
- Onboarding is told as three steps ("Tell us your vibe", "Get matched", "Move in"); field count could not verify.
- Play: 50+ downloads, updated 2 Jul 2026, no rating or reviews. Live listing count could not verify. Publishes
  "best apps" pages ranking itself first, with a disclosure
  (<https://flatmates.360ghar.com/best-flatmate-finder-apps-india>).

### Bunkd — <https://www.getbunkd.in> · Play id `in.getbunkd.app`
- "a free swipe-to-match app for finding a flatmate, a room or a PG in India". Mendly Labs, Bilaspur (HP). PWA
  plus an Android listing.
- "Sign up in 60 seconds": phone number, OTP, profile photo, area. "No long forms, no awkward bio prompts". Then
  lifestyle prompts: five on the homepage (sleep, tidiness, social battery, noise, guests); seven on
  <https://www.getbunkd.in/how-it-works>, including one dealbreaker.
- Roles "Hunter" and "Host"; "Squad Mode lets 2–3 Hunters team up first". Cards show "photos, age, occupation,
  area, rent budget, lifestyle tags, bio" and a "Vibe Score" called "a hint, not gospel". Filters: gender, max
  rent (<https://www.getbunkd.in/faq>).
- "When you both like each other, it is a match and you can chat. Your phone number stays private."
- FAQ says "no paywalls". The refund policy (<https://www.getbunkd.in/refund>) lists subscriptions "Bunkd+, Bunkd
  Gold, Bunkd Platinum, Bunkd Host" and packs "Super Likes, Boosts, Rewinds, Message-Before-Match credits,
  Passport day-passes", and names "unlimited swipes" as a paid benefit. Prices could not verify.
- Trust (<https://www.getbunkd.in/safety>): phone OTP, one account per number, optional email badge, "reports are
  reviewed within 24 hours by a named Grievance Officer", no push "between 10 PM and 8 AM IST", and a red-flag
  list ("never pay anyone before you've seen the flat in person").
- Its own data, 9 Nov 2026 (<https://www.getbunkd.in/flatmate-index>): "689 active profiles across 180 Indian
  cities"; room-seekers Bengaluru 90, Delhi 54, Noida 25, Gurgaon 14.
- Play: 50+ downloads, updated 9 Nov 2026, in-app purchases, no aggregate rating; two 5-star reviews ("I found a
  roommate !!!!!!!!!!", 9 Nov).
- Style: serif display with DM Sans, emoji, a founder story ("Three of us went through the standard Indian
  flatmate hunt"), city pages with sector-level rents (<https://www.getbunkd.in/flatmates-in-gurgaon>).

### NestMate — <https://nestmate.in>
- "Who you live with matters more than where." Delhi NCR and Pune. "not a broker and not a listings site", though

## 4. Patterns that work

- **A named human who follows up.** Flatmate.in's 5-star reviews are mostly about one relationship manager.
  Source: Flatmate.in Play reviews.
- **Chat gated on mutual interest, number private.** All three direct peers lead with it; a NoBroker reviewer
  praises "chat securely before exchanging phone numbers". Sources: getbunkd.in, nestmate.in, NoBroker reviews.
- **A reason beside the score.** "A reason, not just a number" (nestmate.in); "a hint, not gospel"
  (getbunkd.in/faq).
- **Short sign-up, lifestyle after.** Four actions then five to seven prompts (getbunkd.in/how-it-works); four
  fields then eight questions (nestmate.in/register).
- **Saying what verification is not.** "a trust signal, not a background check" (nestmate.in/faq).
- **Visit booking in the product.** "Book visits in 2 taps" (flatmates.360ghar.com); "Schedule a Visit"
  (stanzaliving.com); "easy to use and book viewings" (SpareRoom 5-star review, 25 Oct 2026).
- **Teaming up before finding a flat.** "Squad Mode" (getbunkd.in/faq), "Buddy ups" (spareroom.co.uk), "Team up"
  (roomies.co.in), teams (flatmate.in).
- **Honest local numbers and a stated response time.** Small real counts with a method note
  (getbunkd.in/flatmate-index); reports reviewed within 24 hours (getbunkd.in/safety, nestmate.in/safety).

## 5. Patterns users dislike

Each quote is from the named app's Play reviews, cited in section 3.

- **Paying before you can talk.** "asks for premium to chat with people right from start" (Flatmate.in); "pay to
  contact adds? seriously" (SpareRoom); "First you pay to get the brokers contact number" (MagicBricks).
- **Stale or already-let listings.** "every single property was already rented out" (MagicBricks); "very old data
  is shown" (Flatmate.in); "show same flat for almost 1 month even if they rented out" (Housing).
- **Inactive users and silence.** "useless app with inactive users" (Flatmate.in); "nobody answers you"
  (SpareRoom, 30 Aug 2026).
- **Brokers behind owner listings.** "all the listings are done by agents" (Housing).
- **Calls after sign-up.** "too many calls" (Housing); "after that they start calling" (Zolo).
- **Hidden charges, deposits and refunds.** "so many hidden charges" (Zolo); "they are not giving my deposit
  back" (Stanza); "they lie about the refund part" (Flatmate.in).
- **Cannot hide seen items or flag dead ones.** "no way to see new listings or know what you've already viewed"
  (Flatmate.in); "NO option to report this or mark a property as rented" (MagicBricks).
- **Weak filters.** "no option to filter out based on gender" (Roomies).

## 6. What this means for Find a Flatmate

Everything marked **Opinion** is judgement built on the sources above. None of it has been tested with users.

### (a) Swipe cards versus list and filter
- **Fact.** Bunkd and the 360 FlatMates app market swipe; NestMate uses a ranked feed; every incumbent uses list
  and filter. No review found praises or blames the discovery mechanic; complaints are about paywalls, stale
  supply and silence. Bunkd's swipe deck carries dating-style monetisation ("unlimited swipes", "Rewinds",
  "Message-Before-Match credits").
- **Opinion.** Keep the ranked list. Nothing here contradicts the PRD's "not Tinder for flatmates" stance, and
  "Message-Before-Match" shows how swipe economics erode the mutual-interest promise. Borrow what makes cards feel
  fast: one person or room per card, rent in large type, two or three tags, one sentence of reason, and
  "Interested" / "Not for me" on the card. Add "hide seen" and "new since last visit".

### (b) Text and onboarding length
- **Fact.** Bunkd: phone, OTP, photo, area, then five to seven prompts. NestMate: four fields, then eight
  questions. 360 Flatmates: six dimensions. Ours: 14 lifestyle questions plus basics and privacy steps (PRD H1).
  NestMate's hero is an eight-word headline, one sentence, two buttons and one trust line.
- **Opinion.** We ask the most before showing anything. Show first matches after six to eight answers and ask the
  rest in context, where our missing-information and confidence display already explains why. One sentence per
  onboarding screen.

### (c) What reads as human and trustworthy, versus generic
- **Human, on the evidence.** A named person who answers (Flatmate.in); a 24-hour report promise and named
  Grievance Officer (Bunkd, NestMate); plain statements of what is and is not checked (NestMate); founder story
  and safety advice in the product's own voice (Bunkd); small honest numbers (Bunkd).
- **Generic, on the evidence.** "Trusted & loved by million users" and filler copy (Flatmate.in); urgency tags
  such as "Filling Fast" (Stanza); unexplained badges such as "Partner verified" (MagicBricks).
- **Opinion.** Our rule against invented statistics and extra trust labels is an advantage if the page says so
  plainly. Add one line on who moderates and how fast, a short first-meeting safety list, and, once real, exact
  local counts instead of adjectives.

### (d) Where we differ and where we are behind
| | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Different:** itemised monthly cost and move-in cash on each listing | No people-matching peer shows it per listing; Bunkd has it only as a city article. Zolo and Stanza reviews show charges and deposits are the sore point. |
| **Different:** no paid tier, so nothing gates contact | Flatmate.in and the portals gate contact; Bunkd sells a way round mutual match. |
| **Different:** no people photos; score withheld when information is thin | Bunkd is photo-first. NestMate's page shows a percentage on every card. |
| **Different:** visit address released only around a confirmed visit | Not described by any competitor checked. |
| **Not different:** lifestyle score, mutual-interest chat, reviewed listings, visit scheduling | 360 Flatmates claims all four in Gurugram; NestMate claims the first two in Delhi NCR. |
| **Behind:** verification | All three direct peers verify phone by OTP; we verify email only. |
| **Behind:** reach and re-engagement | Flatmate.in, Bunkd and 360 have Android apps; Bunkd sends push with quiet hours. We have a PWA with no push. |
| **Behind:** supply | Flatmate.in has 5L+ installs; NoBroker has roommate pages for Gurgaon and Noida. We start at zero. |
| **Behind:** teaming up, findability, dark theme | Four products let seekers group first; Bunkd and 360 publish city guides; NestMate and 360 declare dark themes. |

### Priorities (opinion)
1. **Shorten the path to first results** to about eight answers; ask the rest later.
2. **Make cards decisive, not swipeable:** one-tap interest, hide seen, new since last visit.
3. **Lead with the cost receipt and "nothing is paid, nothing is gated"**; no peer checked offers both.
4. **Put a human face on moderation:** who reviews, how fast, what "moderator reviewed" does and does not mean.
5. **Close the verification gap** with phone OTP, or explain plainly why email only.
6. **Solve silence before scale:** show recent activity and expire unconfirmed listings; "inactive users" is the
   freshest complaint about the nearest incumbent.
7. **Later:** seeker groups, local guides from our own data, dark theme, push.
