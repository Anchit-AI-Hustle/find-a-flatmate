# Competitor Analysis

_Checked 12 November 2026 · Desk research from public pages only. Nothing here comes from signing in, installing
an app or talking to users._

**How to read this.** Every fact is what a public page showed on the date checked, with its URL. Vendor claims are
marked as claims. Play Store figures are from the India storefront (`hl=en_IN&gl=IN`); `L` is lakh, `Cr` is crore.
Review quotes are verbatim (typos kept) from each app's public Play Store reviews, newest first, with the review
date; reviewer and staff names are left out. "Could not verify" means the page did not load or did not say.
Opinions are labelled **Opinion**.

## 1. Landscape

Indian flat-seekers have four kinds of option, and none does the whole job. **Large portals** (NoBroker,
Housing.com, MagicBricks) have the supply and tens of lakhs of ratings, but discovery is list-and-filter over
property cards and contact sits behind paid plans; recent 1-star reviews are about stale listings, brokers posing as
owners and sales calls. **Managed co-living operators** (Stanza Living, Zolo) sell a bed, not a flatmate; their
recent 1-star reviews are about deposits and charges. **Flatmate.in** is the established flatmate-specific app
(5L+ installs, Noida-based operator); its praise is almost all for human relationship managers and its complaints
are about paying before you can chat, inactive users and refunds. A **new wave of small compatibility-first
products** (Bunkd, 360 Flatmates, NestMate) launched with nearly the same promise as ours: lifestyle questions, a
match score, chat only after mutual interest, no brokerage. Two of them market swipe cards. All three are tiny on
the evidence they publish themselves: Bunkd reports 14 room-seekers in Gurgaon and 25 in Noida, and the Bunkd and
360 FlatMates Android apps each show "50+" downloads. Facebook groups and Reddit could not be loaded, so the
informal channel is described only through secondary sources.

## 3. Notes per competitor

All pages checked 12 Nov 2026.

### Flatmate.in
- **What.** "Find Flat and Flatmates, Rooms on Rent, Shared Apartments". Product of Think Straight IT LLP, Noida.
  Also sells rental agreements, tenant verification and a "List Your PG" service. <https://flatmate.in>
- **Discovery.** The Gurgaon page is a list of cards: first name, locality, rent, "Looking for Male/Female
  Roommate", distance. Tabs for All / Rooms / Roommates / PG and a gender filter. Cards are visible without
  signing in. The homepage also claims "compatibility match percentages" and team creation for people searching
  the same locality. <https://www.flatmate.in/flatmates-in-gurgaon>
- **Contact and price.** Homepage: "secure chat feature", option to hide your number, and an "RM Plan" where
  relationship managers search for you. App Store India lists one in-app purchase, "FlatMate.in Premium (One
  Month)" at ₹499. <https://apps.apple.com/in/app/flatmate-find-roommate-rooms/id1360260090>
- **Copy.** Claims "Trusted & loved by million users" and "With 1000+ listings". The long how-it-works text is
  awkward ("possess the authority to search into your favored location").
- **Stores.** Play: 3.9 from 3,648 ratings, 5L+ downloads, updated 5 Nov 2026, contains ads. App Store India: 3.1
  from 215 ratings. <https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.flatmate&hl=en_IN&gl=IN>
- **Review themes (40 newest Play reviews, 17 Aug – 10 Nov 2026).** Most 5-star reviews thank a named
  relationship manager: "i was assigned a dedicated relationship manager" (24 Oct). 1-star reviews: "useless app
  with inactive users" (10 Nov); "I wasn't allowed to have a conversation with anyone as soon as I started
  searching" (9 Nov); "the app asks for premium to chat with people right from start" (4 stars, 30 Oct); "very old
  data is shown" (6 Oct); "no way to see new listings or know what you've already viewed" (2 Oct); "they lie about
  the refund part" (26 Aug).

### 360 Flatmates
- **What.** "Find Compatible Flatmates & Verified Rooms Across India". "We're live in Gurugram and Bangalore right
  now." By 360Ghar. <https://flatmates.360ghar.com>
- **Matching.** "6-dimension compatibility scoring — sleep, cleanliness, food, guests, work, and lifestyle", plus
  "society vibe tags". The Play listing adds "swipe-based matching" and "map-based discovery", and says landlords
  and property managers can list. <https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.the360ghar.flatmates360&hl=en_IN&gl=IN>
- **Contact.** "Book visits in 2 taps — no WhatsApp ping-pong." "Chat that starts with context — every chat
  already carries the listing, the match score, and visit details."
- **Trust and price.** "every room is reviewed before it goes live. Real rent, real availability."; "phone OTP,
  profile checks, and in-app reporting". "Searching and matching is 100% free. Optional paid plans exist for
  priority listings."
- **Onboarding.** Told as three steps: "Tell us your vibe", "Get matched", "Move in". Field count could not
  verify (the app is client-rendered).
- **Scale.** Play: 50+ downloads, updated 2 Jul 2026, no rating or reviews shown. Live listing count could not
  verify. It publishes "best flatmate finder apps" pages that rank itself first, with a disclosure.
  <https://flatmates.360ghar.com/best-flatmate-finder-apps-india>
- **Style.** Short, casual lines ("night owl or a 6 AM gym person"); light and dark theme colours declared.

### Bunkd
- **What.** "a free swipe-to-match app for finding a flatmate, a room or a PG in India". Made by Mendly Labs,
  Bilaspur, Himachal Pradesh. Runs as a PWA; an Android listing exists. <https://www.getbunkd.in>
- **Onboarding.** "Sign up in 60 seconds": phone number, OTP, profile photo, area. "No long forms, no awkward bio
  prompts". Then lifestyle prompts: the homepage says five (sleep, tidiness, social battery, noise, guests); the
  how-it-works page lists seven including food, one dealbreaker and an "Unpopular opinion".
  <https://www.getbunkd.in/how-it-works>
- **Matching.** Roles are "Hunter" (seeker) and "Host" (has a room). "Squad Mode lets 2–3 Hunters team up first".
  Cards show "photos, age, occupation, area, rent budget, lifestyle tags, bio" and a "Vibe Score" described as
  "a hint, not gospel". Filters: gender and max rent. <https://www.getbunkd.in/faq>
- **Contact.** "When you both like each other, it is a match and you can chat. Your phone number stays private."
- **Price.** FAQ says "No listing fees, no brokerage, no paywalls." The refund policy lists subscriptions
  ("Bunkd+, Bunkd Gold, Bunkd Platinum, Bunkd Host") and packs ("Super Likes, Boosts, Rewinds,
  Message-Before-Match credits, Passport day-passes"), and names "unlimited swipes" as a paid benefit. Prices
  could not verify. <https://www.getbunkd.in/refund>
- **Trust.** Phone OTP, one account per number, optional email badge, block and report, "reports are reviewed
  within 24 hours by a named Grievance Officer", no push "between 10 PM and 8 AM IST", and a red-flag list in its
  own voice ("never pay anyone before you've seen the flat in person"). <https://www.getbunkd.in/safety>
- **Scale (its own data, dated 9 Nov 2026).** "689 active profiles across 180 Indian cities"; room-seekers:
  Bengaluru 90, Delhi 54, Noida 25, Gurgaon 14. <https://www.getbunkd.in/flatmate-index>
- **Stores.** Play: 50+ downloads, updated 9 Nov 2026, in-app purchases, no aggregate rating; two 5-star reviews
  ("I found a roommate !!!!!!!!!!", 9 Nov). <https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.getbunkd.app&hl=en_IN&gl=IN>
- **Style.** Serif display with DM Sans, emoji, founder story ("Three of us went through the standard Indian
  flatmate hunt"), city pages with sector-level rent ranges and an all-in monthly cost table.
  <https://www.getbunkd.in/flatmates-in-gurgaon>

### NestMate
- **What.** "Who you live with matters more than where." Delhi NCR and Pune. "NestMate is not a broker and not a
  listings site", though the FAQ allows one optional room listing per user. <https://nestmate.in>
- **Onboarding.** Register form has four fields (full name, email, password, confirm password), then "Eight
  questions about the things that actually cause friction". <https://nestmate.in/register>
- **Matching.** Published weights: cleanliness 20%, sleep 15%, guests 15%, smoking 15%, diet 10%, drinking 10%,
  noise and social 10%, pets 5%. Each person shows a percentage, "a plain sentence explaining what you have in
  common" and an "eight-petal mark". "Name up to three things you will not live with."
- **Contact.** "Messaging opens only once you have both agreed to it."
- **Trust.** "Every profile shows exactly what has been checked — phone, email — in plain words, not a badge you
  have to decode." FAQ: "Photo and ID verification are on our roadmap but not live yet. Verification is a trust
  signal, not a background check". Reports reviewed "usually within 24 hours"; a "Paused" status hides a profile
  without deleting it. <https://nestmate.in/faq>
- **Price and app.** "NestMate is currently free." No app-store listing found. User count could not verify.
- **Style.** Minimal and text-led; sample card uses initials, not a photo; light and dark themes declared.

## 4. Patterns that work

- **A named human who follows up.** Flatmate.in's recent 5-star reviews are mostly about one relationship manager
  ("i was assigned a dedicated relationship manager"). Source: Flatmate.in Play reviews.
- **Chat gated on mutual interest, number kept private.** Bunkd, NestMate and 360 Flatmates all lead with it; a
  NoBroker reviewer praises "chat securely before exchanging phone numbers". Sources: getbunkd.in, nestmate.in,
  NoBroker Play reviews.
- **A reason next to the score.** NestMate: "A reason, not just a number". Bunkd calls its score "a hint, not
  gospel". Sources: nestmate.in, getbunkd.in/faq.
- **Very short sign-up, lifestyle after.** Bunkd: four actions then five to seven prompts. NestMate: four fields
  then eight questions. Sources: getbunkd.in/how-it-works, nestmate.in/register.
- **Saying plainly what verification is and is not.** NestMate: "a trust signal, not a background check". Source:
  nestmate.in/faq.
- **Visit booking inside the product.** 360 Flatmates ("Book visits in 2 taps"), Stanza ("Schedule a Visit"), and
  a SpareRoom 5-star review ("easy to use and book viewings"). Sources: flatmates.360ghar.com, stanzaliving.com,
  SpareRoom Play reviews.
- **Teaming up before finding a flat.** Bunkd "Squad Mode", SpareRoom "Buddy ups", Roomies "Team up", Flatmate.in
  team creation. Sources: getbunkd.in/faq, spareroom.co.uk, roomies.co.in, flatmate.in.
- **Honest, local numbers.** Bunkd publishes its small counts and sector-level rent ranges with a method note.
  Source: getbunkd.in/flatmate-index.
- **A stated response time for reports.** Bunkd and NestMate both promise review within 24 hours. Sources:
  getbunkd.in/safety, nestmate.in/safety.

## 5. Patterns users dislike

- **Paying before you can talk to anyone.** "asks for premium to chat with people right from start"
  (Flatmate.in); "pay to contact adds? seriously" (SpareRoom); "First you pay to get the brokers contact number"
  (MagicBricks). Sources: Play reviews of each.
- **Stale or already-let listings.** "every single property was already rented out" (MagicBricks); "show same
  flat for almost 1 month even if they rented out" (Housing); "very old data is shown" (Flatmate.in).
- **Inactive users and no replies.** "useless app with inactive users" (Flatmate.in); "nobody answers you"
  (SpareRoom, 30 Aug 2026).
- **Brokers behind owner listings.** "all the listings are done by agents" (Housing); "doesn't verify their
  brokers and their properties" (Housing).
- **Sales and spam calls after sign-up.** "too many calls" (Housing); "after that they start calling" (Zolo).
- **Hidden charges and deposits not returned.** "so many hidden charges" (Zolo); "they are not giving my deposit
  back" (Stanza).
- **Refund promises not kept.** "they lie about the refund part" (Flatmate.in).
- **No way to hide what you have seen or flag a dead listing.** "no way to see new listings or know what you've
  already viewed" (Flatmate.in); "NO option to report this or mark a property as rented" (MagicBricks).
- **Weak filters.** "no option to filter out based on gender" (Roomies).

## 6. What this means for Find a Flatmate

Everything in this section is **opinion** built on the sources above. None of it has been tested with users.

### (a) Swipe cards versus list and filter
- **Fact.** Bunkd and the 360 FlatMates app market swipe. NestMate uses a ranked feed. Every incumbent uses list
  and filter. No review found praises or blames the discovery mechanic itself; complaints are about paywalls,
  stale supply and silence. Bunkd's swipe deck comes with dating-style monetisation (paid "unlimited swipes",
  "Rewinds", "Message-Before-Match credits").
- **Opinion.** Keep the ranked list; the PRD's "not Tinder for flatmates" stance is not contradicted by anything
  here, and Bunkd's "Message-Before-Match" shows how swipe economics erode the mutual-interest promise. Borrow
  what makes cards feel fast: one person or room per card, rent in large type, two or three tags, one sentence of
  reason, and "Interested" / "Not for me" on the card itself. Add "hide seen" and "new since last visit".

### (b) Text and onboarding length
- **Fact.** Bunkd: phone, OTP, photo, area, then five to seven prompts. NestMate: four fields, then eight
  questions. 360 Flatmates: six dimensions. Ours: 14 lifestyle questions plus basics and privacy steps (PRD H1).
  NestMate's hero is a nine-word headline, one sentence, two buttons and one trust line.
- **Opinion.** We ask the most before showing anything. Show first matches after about six to eight answers and
  ask the rest in context, where our "missing info" and confidence display already explains why it helps. Cut
  landing and onboarding copy to one sentence per screen.

### (c) What reads as human and trustworthy, versus generic
- **Human, on the evidence.** A named person who answers (Flatmate.in); a stated 24-hour report review and named
  Grievance Officer (Bunkd, NestMate); plain statements of what is checked and what is not (NestMate); a founder
  story and safety advice in the product's own voice (Bunkd); small honest numbers (Bunkd).
- **Generic, on the evidence.** "Trusted & loved by million users" and awkward filler copy (Flatmate.in); urgency
  tags such as "Filling Fast" (Stanza); badges with no explanation ("Partner verified", MagicBricks).
- **Opinion.** Our rules already forbid invented statistics and extra trust labels; that is an advantage if the
  page says so in plain words. Add: who moderates and how fast, in one line; a short first-meeting safety list;
  and, once real, exact local counts ("n rooms in Sector 45 this week") instead of adjectives.

### (d) Where we differ and where we are behind
| | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Different: itemised monthly cost and move-in cash on each listing** | No people-matching peer shows it per listing; Bunkd has it only as a city article. Zolo and Stanza reviews show charges and deposits are the sore point. |
| **Different: no paid tier, so nothing gates contact** | Flatmate.in and the portals gate contact; Bunkd sells a way round mutual match. |
| **Different: no people photos; score withheld when information is thin** | Bunkd is photo-first. NestMate shows reasons but always a percentage. |
| **Different: visit address released only around a confirmed visit** | Not described by any competitor checked. |
| **Similar, not different: lifestyle score, mutual-interest chat, reviewed listings, visit scheduling** | 360 Flatmates claims all four in Gurugram; NestMate claims the first two in Delhi NCR. |
| **Behind: verification** | All three direct peers verify phone by OTP; we verify email only. |
| **Behind: reach and re-engagement** | Flatmate.in, Bunkd and 360 have Android apps; Bunkd sends push with quiet hours. We have a PWA with no push. |
| **Behind: supply** | Flatmate.in has 5L+ installs and NoBroker has roommate pages for Gurgaon and Noida. We start at zero. |
| **Behind: teaming up** | Four products let seekers group before finding a flat; we do not. |
| **Behind: findability** | Bunkd and 360 publish city guides and comparison pages; we have none. |
| **Behind: dark theme** | NestMate and 360 Flatmates declare one; we are light only. |

### Priorities (opinion)
1. **Shorten the path to first results** to about eight answers; ask the rest later.
2. **Make cards decisive, not swipeable:** one-tap interest on the card, hide seen, new-since-last-visit.
3. **Lead with the cost receipt and the "nothing is paid, nothing is gated" promise**; no peer can copy both.
4. **Put a human face on moderation:** who reviews, how fast, what "moderator reviewed" does and does not mean.
5. **Close the verification gap** (phone OTP) or explain plainly why email only.
6. **Solve silence before scale:** show recent activity and expire unconfirmed listings; "inactive users" is the
   top complaint about the nearest incumbent.
7. **Later:** seeker groups, sector-level local guides built from our own data, dark theme, push.
